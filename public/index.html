The Calm Companion Program

Private dog training for a calmer, easier life together.

For families overwhelmed by their dog’s chaotic behavior, the Calm Companion Program is an eight-session private training program designed to turn your dog from a daily source of stress into a calmer, more enjoyable part of the family.

Together we’ll build clearer communication, better everyday behavior, and a relationship where you can increasingly trust your dog to make the right choices. Every program is tailored to your dog, your goals, and your real life — there is no cookie-cutter curriculum.

The problem we’re solving

Designed for owners dealing with everyday behavioral problems such as:

  • Pulling on leash
  • Chaotic or overly excitable behavior
  • Difficulty settling
  • Mild-to-moderate dog reactivity
  • Poor attention around distractions
  • Difficulty communicating clear expectations
  • Feeling like you’re constantly managing your dog

These issues may not sound extreme on paper. But when you’re dealing with them every day, they add up — walks, guests, seeing another dog, even relaxing at home. The program is designed to change those everyday patterns.

Who it’s for

This program is a good fit for the owner who is thinking:

“I know my dog is capable of better. I just don’t know how to get there.”

You don’t need previous training experience. You do need to be willing to participate. This is hands-on private coaching — I’ll teach you what to do, explain why we’re doing it, and help you improve. The progress ultimately comes from the relationship you build with your dog.

The program is not intended for severe behavioral cases involving serious human-directed aggression, dog fighting, or other dangerous behavior.

What you’ll receive

Eight private training sessions, generally scheduled no more than once per week. Whenever possible, we train where the behavior actually matters:

  • Your home
  • Your neighborhood or normal walking route
  • A nearby park or another place you regularly visit

Training should transfer into your real life — not exist only during a lesson. You’ll also receive:

  • Written notes following sessions
  • Supporting training videos when useful
  • Text and email support between lessons

What you’ll learn

Rather than a checklist of commands, we work on the skills your particular dog and household actually need, such as:

  • Clearer communication
  • Leash handling & calmer walking
  • Engagement and attention
  • Better choices around distractions
  • Appropriate outlets for energy
  • Household boundaries and helping your dog settle
  • Managing and improving mild-to-moderate reactivity
  • When and how to intervene on a poor choice

Most importantly, you’ll learn why you’re doing what you’re doing — so the training makes sense to you, not so you blindly follow instructions.

The outcome

Eight sessions aren’t about a dog who performs perfectly for a trainer. They’re about giving you a framework you can keep using after the program is finished — so life starts feeling different:

Less embarrassment. Less worrying. Less managing. More confidence. More trust. More freedom.
